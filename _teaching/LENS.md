During my PhD, I spent four months at the **European Laboratory for Nonlinear Spectroscopy (LENS)** in the group of Prof. **Diederik Wiersma**, within the framework of the European Network of Excellence **PHOREMOST**.

During this research stay in Florence, I explored the role of **structural disorder in time-domain light transport** through nanophotonic structures. This work directly connected multiple scattering, resonant feedback, and optical gain, and led to several joint publications in high-impact journals.
