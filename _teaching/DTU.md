In the **Quantum Photonics** group led by Prof. **Peter Lodahl**, I focused on the role of **fabrication imperfections** in state-of-the-art III–V semiconductor nanostructures for quantum optics.

These structures — photonic-crystal waveguides and photonic crystals embedding quantum-light emitters — are widely used for quantum optics experiments. During this period, I became an expert in **micro-photoluminescence under cryogenic conditions**, as well as in numerical modeling of nanophotonic structures using **plane-wave expansion** and **finite-difference time-domain (FDTD)** simulations.
