--- layout: archive title: "Experience" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- **Professional journey** My scientific path has been shaped by a sequence of cities, people, and research cultures across Europe. I began in **Madrid**, where I trained as a materials scientist and nanophotonics researcher, working on complex photonic structures and disorder-driven light transport. I then moved to **Copenhagen**, where my work shifted toward quantum and mesoscopic nanophotonics, and where I discovered the power of imperfections as a physical resource rather than a limitation. A strong and lasting connection to **Italy**, especially **Florence**, introduced me to the broader physics of complexity, multiple scattering, and nonlinear light–matter interaction, deeply influencing my scientific perspective. I later settled in **Barcelona**, where my research expanded toward optomechanics, nonlinear dynamics, and emergent phenomena in hybrid photonic systems.
